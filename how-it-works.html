How the Saddle Library Works

Our goal is to get more people riding with the right saddle.

1. Choose a Saddle

Browse the available saddles and request the one you want to try.

2. Wait for Confirmation

We'll double check that the saddle is ready, and let you know when and where to pick it up. The library is located in the Manayunk section of Philadelphia.

3. Schedule Pickup

You will receive an email confirming the saddle is available. We'll place it in the dropbox for you to pick up. Pickups are generally available between 9am and 8pm, but we can work with you if you need it another time.

4. Test Ride and Return

Borrow period is three weeks. When you're done, return the saddle to the dropbox. Please make sure the saddle is clean and in good condition for the next rider.

Some saddles are available for purchase directly from the library. If you'd like to purchase your saddle please reach out to us for more information.
